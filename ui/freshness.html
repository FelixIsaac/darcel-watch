Freshness

Not “what is broken?” — “what has expired?”

Broken is provably wrong right now: a phone row with no number, hours that close before they open. The rest of this app hunts those. Stale is different. The listing may well be fine — but nothing in the record says anyone confirmed it against reality. That is not an error, it is a shelf life. Every field carries an implicit promise that decays: a phone number stays true for years, opening hours for months. This page scores that decay.

ShelterTech maintain this directory actively: 808 of 813 approved listings were updated in the last 90 days. What the data lacks is provenance — updated_at says something changed, not that it was checked. So the score below is low mostly because almost nothing carries evidence stronger than “well-formed”, not because the directory is neglected.

Model and constants: freshness.py. Source directory: sfserviceguide.org (nonprofit ShelterTech).

The number that should move Median freshness across every approved listing. One number, tracked over time — if the work is working, this goes up.

Why hours expire faster than phone numbers Confidence in a confirmed value halves after one half-life, and keeps halving. Opening hours change with seasons, funding and staffing; an organisation keeps its number for years.

These half-lives are our judgement, not a measurement. They were never fitted to data. The right way to set them is to mine ShelterTech’s own change-request history for how often each field actually changes. The same applies to the field weights.

The evidence ladder What kind of evidence last supported a value sets its ceiling. Age then decays it from there. The ceilings, like the half-lives, are our judgement calls rather than measurements.

A structural check proves a value is well-formed, never that it is current. “(415) 555-0123” is a perfectly well-formed number for an organisation that closed in 2019. That is why structural_ok is capped at 40 and can never climb higher no matter how recently the record was touched. Only a human who checked, or agreement with the organisation’s own source, resets the clock.

The work plan The lowest-scoring listings, each with the single cheapest action that would raise it most. 800 stale listings is useless; 800 stale listings with one named next action each is a plan.

What would it take? Arithmetic on our own model — not a promise, and not a measurement.
